# Household App — User Guide

## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** at the login screen, or sign in if you already have one.
2. **Set up your household** — give it a name. You'll get an invite code.
3. **Share the invite code** with family/roommates so they can join the same household. Everyone shares the same pantry, recipes, meal plan, and shopping list.

---

## Pantry

Track everything you have at home.

- Tap **+ Add Item** to add something manually — fill in name, quantity, unit, category, storage location, and optionally an expiration date and price.
- Items with expiration dates show a color-coded badge: red = expired or expiring very soon, yellow = expiring this week.
- Use the **search bar** or **category filter** to find items quickly.
- Tap **Edit** on any item to update it, or **Delete** to remove it.

### Scanning a Receipt

The fastest way to populate your pantry:

1. Go to **Receipts** and tap **+ Scan New Receipt**.
2. Take a photo of your receipt (or upload one from your camera roll).
3. The app uses AI to extract all items, prices, and the store name automatically.
4. Review the list — edit names, quantities, categories, or prices as needed. Uncheck anything you don't want to add.
5. Add any discounts/coupons and link them to the relevant item.
6. Tap **Add to pantry** — done.

---

## Recipes

Your personal recipe book.

- Tap **+ Add Recipe** to create one from scratch with ingredients and instructions.
- Tap **Import Recipe** to paste text copied from any website — the app parses it automatically.
- Tap **Cookbook** to photograph a cookbook page and have the recipe extracted via AI.
- Tap any recipe to view it. From the detail view you can:
  - Edit or delete it
  - Rate it (1–5 stars)
  - Cook it (which logs leftovers automatically)

---

## Meal Plan

Plan your week's meals and scale recipes.

- The calendar shows the current week. Use the arrows to navigate forward or back.
- Tap **+ Add** on any day to assign a recipe as breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a snack.
- Tap a meal to expand it and see:
  - **Scale** — switch between 1/2x, 1x, or 2x servings
  - **Ingredients** — quantities adjust automatically with the scale, or edit any individually
  - **Pantry status** — ingredients you already have are highlighted; missing ones show an **+ List** button to add them directly to your shopping list
- Tap **Mark cooked** when you've made a meal.

---

## Shopping List

- Tap **+ Add** to add items manually.
- Tap **Generate from this week's meal plan** to automatically add any ingredients your meal plan needs that aren't already in your pantry.
- Items are grouped by category for easier shopping.
- Check off items as you shop — checked items move to an "In cart" section.
- Tap **Add to pantry** on the checked section to move everything you bought into your pantry at once.
- Tap **Clear checked** to remove them from the list without adding to pantry.

---

## Suggestions

See which recipes you can make right now.

- Recipes are ranked by how many of their ingredients you already have in your pantry.
- Only recipes with 50%+ ingredient match are shown.
- Items expiring soon are highlighted in orange — recipes that use them are flagged as "Uses expiring."
- Tap a recipe card to go to the Recipes page and find it.

---

## Nutrition

Log what you eat and track daily macros.

- Search the USDA food database for any food item.
- Select a result and enter the number of servings, then tap **Log**.
- The top section shows today's calorie ring and macro progress bars (protein, carbs, fat).
- Tap **View log** to see everything logged today, with per-item calorie counts.
- Tap **Edit goals** to set your personal daily targets for calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and fiber.

---

## Leftovers

After cooking a recipe, leftovers are tracked here automatically.

- Each leftover shows the recipe it came from, remaining servings, and expiration date.
- Use the **+** / **-** buttons to update remaining servings as you eat them.
- Tap **X** to remove a leftover when it's gone or discarded.

---

## Analytics

View spending trends over time.

- **Weekly spending** chart shows the last 8 weeks of grocery spend (sourced from receipt scans).
- **Spending by store** breaks down where your money goes.
- **Price comparison** — search any item to see which store had the best price based on your receipt history.

---

## Tips

- The **Dashboard** shows a quick summary: total pantry items, what's expiring soon, and expired items. It also shows your household invite code.
- Expiring items on the dashboard link directly to Suggestions so you can use them up before they go bad.
- Receipt scanning works best with good lighting and a flat, uncrumpled receipt.
- Cookbook photo scanning works best when the page is well-lit and the text isn't cut off.
